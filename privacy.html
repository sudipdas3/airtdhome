AIR Teachers' Day 2K26

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the AIR Teachers' Day 2K26 registration portal collects, uses, and protects information from participants who register for the event.

Information we collect

When you register through the portal, we may collect:

  • Contact details — name, email address, and phone number.
  • Institutional details — school or department name and role.
  • Event preferences — sessions or activities you select during registration.

How we use your information

Registration information is used to confirm your participation, send event updates, coordinate logistics, and share materials related to AIR Teachers' Day 2K26. We do not use your information for unrelated marketing.

Sharing of information

Your information is shared only with the organizing team and volunteers directly involved in running the event. We do not sell or rent participant data to third parties.

Data retention

Registration records are retained only as long as needed to manage the event and follow-up communications, after which they are securely deleted.

Your choices

You may request to review, correct, or delete the information you submitted by contacting the organizing team.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the AIR Teachers' Day 2K26 organizing committee.
